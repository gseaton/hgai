- **docs/architecture/sparql-vs-shql-gaps.md** — Prioritization analysis for the SPARQL capabilities SHQL lacks (per the earlier "SPARQL to SHQL Conversion Plan" artifact's comparison table). Scores each gap on real-world usage frequency, engineering cost/location (transpiler-only vs. SHQL-engine change vs. RDF-import change), and whether an adequate workaround already exists. Introduces a cross-cutting "push computation into the transpiler" strategy (client-side aggregation/BIND/ASK over a broad SHQL fetch, capped by SHQL's internal 2,000-candidate-per-pattern search limit) that reclassifies several gaps from "engine change" to "transpiler-only." Concludes with five priority tiers (build now: `VALUES`, numeric aggregates, output-only `BIND`; build if cheap: `ASK`, inverse/fixed-length property paths; approximate only: `FILTER EXISTS`/`MINUS`; explicitly out of scope: subqueries, `CONSTRUCT`/`DESCRIBE`, per-pattern `GRAPH`, `SERVICE`, SPARQL Update, general property-path algebra; a separate import-time-not-query-time tier: literal datatype/language preservation), and maps the tiers back onto the earlier plan's phases.
